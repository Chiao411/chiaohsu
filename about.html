嗨，我是 ChiaoHi, I'm Chiao

一個喜歡分享藝術與金融科技的人。音樂出身、編輯養成，最近在幣圈想著支付和 AI agent。Someone who likes sharing art and fintech. Trained in music, shaped by editing, and lately thinking about payments and AI agents in crypto.

我其實是音樂出身。練琴、聽音色、在意節奏，這些習慣到現在都還在，只是換到了文字上。

I actually started in music. Practicing, listening for tone, caring about rhythm: those habits stayed with me. They just moved over to writing.

後來我成了編輯，開始大量接觸視覺藝術和科技藝術。編輯這份工作教會我一件事：再複雜的東西，都值得被好好說清楚。

Then I became an editor and spent a lot of time with visual and tech art. Editing taught me one thing: even the most complicated idea deserves to be explained well.

我想做的，是把不太好懂的東西，轉譯成大家讀得下去的文字。What I want to do is translate the hard-to-grasp into words people want to read.

這幾年我轉進幣圈，最近腦袋裡轉的多半是支付，還有 AI agent 會怎麼改變我們花錢、工作和創作的方式。

In the past few years I moved into crypto. Lately my head is full of payments, and of how AI agents might change the way we spend, work and make things.

這個網站是我的作品集，也是我的電子名片。你會在這裡看到我寫的東西，從藝術慢慢長到金融科技。

This site is my portfolio and my digital business card. You'll find my writing here, slowly growing from art toward fintech.

一篇文章是怎麼長出來的How a piece comes together

從一個念頭到收進蟻丘，大概會經過這四步。From a passing thought to a spot in the anthill, it usually goes like this.

  1. 撿到Spot it

    看展、讀新聞、跟人聊天時，被某個東西卡住。At a show, in the news, in a chat, something snags my attention.

  2. 挖下去Dig in

    查資料、找脈絡，弄清楚它為什麼重要。Research and context, until I know why it matters.

  3. 嚼一嚼Chew it over

    用編輯的手把它拆開、重組，寫成好讀的版本。Take it apart and rebuild it with an editor's hands, into something readable.

  4. 收進蟻丘Bring it home

    發布之後收進這裡，不再散落各地。Publish it and file it here, so nothing gets scattered again.
